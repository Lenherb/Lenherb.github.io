DoomDuck – Privacy Policy

Developer: Lenherb · Effective date: 14 October 2026

Summary

DoomDuck is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in. Your progress is stored only on your own device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • game progress: completed levels, difficulty, stars and highscores;
  • coins, purchased upgrades, unlocked skills and the selected skin;
  • settings such as music and sound volume and joystick options;
  • how many free rewarded ads you watched today.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to get free coins or to revive after dying. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • your IP address and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this data. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time it starts, using Google's consent message. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or manage your choices in detail.

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising and for storing or reading information on your device; legitimate interest for showing non-personalised ads and preventing fraud, where allowed.
  • Changing your choice: you can change or withdraw your consent at any time via Options → Privacy settings in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The game does not currently sell anything for real money. If in-app purchases are added later, payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

DoomDuck is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google for advertising data; we will gladly help you with any question. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Belgium: the Data Protection Authority).

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version.
