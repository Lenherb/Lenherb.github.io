DoomDuck – Privacy Policy

Developer: Lenherb · Effective date: 20 October 2026

Summary

DoomDuck is a single-player game. It has no accounts of its own and no chat. Your progress is stored only on your own device. The game uses four Google services, each described below: optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob), usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), an optional online leaderboard (Google Play Games) and, if you buy something, payments through Google Play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device so you can continue playing. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • game progress: completed levels, difficulty, stars and highscores;
  • coins, purchased upgrades, unlocked skills and the selected skin;
  • settings such as music and sound volume and joystick options;
  • how many free rewarded ads you watched today;
  • your kill count for the online leaderboard.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to get free coins or to revive after dying. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • your IP address and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this data. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it (for example which levels are too hard), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects:

  • an app-instance ID (a random identifier for this installation of the game) and, only if you consent, your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and an approximate (country/region-level) location derived from your IP address;
  • usage events: first launch, sessions and screens viewed, in-app purchases, and game events we send ourselves: a level started or completed (level number and difficulty), a revive (paid with coins or an ad) and a fully watched rewarded ad (what it was for).

We only see these statistics in aggregated reports in the Firebase console; they do not contain your name, e-mail address or Play Games profile. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics follows the consent choice described below: without consent, no identifiers are stored and only limited, cookieless signals are sent.

Online leaderboard (Google Play Games)

The game has an online "Total Kills" leaderboard provided by Google Play Games Services. When the game starts, it tries to sign you in to Google Play Games automatically; you can also sign in with the button on the leaderboard screen. If you are signed in:

  • your Play Games player ID and your gamer tag (Play Games display name) are used to show you on the leaderboard; Google's own leaderboard screen ("View all") may also show your Play Games profile picture;
  • your total number of kills is sent to Google and shown, together with your gamer tag, to other players on the leaderboard;
  • the game reads the top scores and the gamer tags of other players to show the leaderboard.

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive or store it ourselves. Using the leaderboard is optional: if you do not sign in, nothing is sent. You can change your gamer tag, your profile visibility or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings on your device (or at play.google.com/games/profile).

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time it starts, using Google's consent message. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or manage your choices in detail. The same choice also applies to usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase).

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising, for usage statistics and for storing or reading information on your device; legitimate interest for showing non-personalised ads and preventing fraud, where allowed.
  • Changing your choice: you can change or withdraw your consent at any time via Options → Privacy settings in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never receive your payment details; Google Play only tells the game which product was bought so the coins can be added on your device.

Children

DoomDuck is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. We do not store your personal data ourselves; usage statistics are processed by Google on our behalf, and we will handle any request about them. For advertising, leaderboard and payment data, requests are best directed to Google; we will gladly help you with any question. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Belgium: the Data Protection Authority).

Delete your data

To request deletion of your data, send an e-mail to support.doomduck@gmail.com with the subject "DoomDuck data deletion". We will reply within 30 days and tell you what was deleted.

  • Game data on your device (progress, coins, settings, kill count): you can delete this yourself at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the game. It is never stored anywhere else.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): on request we delete the data linked to your app-instance ID. Otherwise it is deleted automatically after 2 months; only aggregated, anonymous reports are kept.
  • Leaderboard data (Google Play Games): your score and gamer tag are stored by Google. You can delete them by deleting your Play Games profile or this game's data in the Google Play Games settings (play.google.com/games/profile).
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): this is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings and manage your data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Purchases: records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play as required for payments and tax law.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are kept at user level for 2 months and then only in aggregated reports. Other data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game changes. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version.
